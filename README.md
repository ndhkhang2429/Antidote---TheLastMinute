## Story & Lore

**"The higher you climb, the deeper the nightmare."**

In the aftermath of a catastrophic bio-hazard event, the city has been quarantined, leaving its remaining citizens trapped in an evolving nightmare. A mysterious, highly reactive toxic fluid has flooded the lower levels, forcing survivors to seek refuge on the rooftops. However, the rooftops are no longer safe. The infection has mutated the fallen into aggressive, unpredictable horrors that roam the concrete peaks. 

You play as a lone survivor trapped in this high-altitude hell. Surrounded by a chilling atmosphere and relentless infected, you must navigate the treacherous, rain-slicked environments, manage your limited resources, and find an extraction point. The fluid is rising, the zombies are mutating, and your only way out is forward.
